title: Molds & Stamps
order: 10
icon: "🌀"
summary: Optic, pineapple, and fin molds for patterning gathers, plus stamp molds for pressing motifs into bits.
---
Optic molds rib or pattern a gather before it's blown out. Pineapple molds give a raised diamond texture. Fin molds (raggilume) are small lampworking molds. Stamp molds (fracchi) press faces, animals, and other motifs into a hot bit.
