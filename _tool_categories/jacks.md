---
title: Jacks
order: 1
icon: "🗜️"
summary: The spring-steel tongs used to shape, neck, and open hot glass.
---
Jacks are the glassblower's most-used shaping tool: two blades joined by a spring handle, used to make necks, form jack lines, and open up lips. Blades come in steel, graphite, or wood, and the shape and length change what a pair is good at.
