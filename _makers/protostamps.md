Protostamps, run by Samwich Glassworks in Taos, cuts brass and graphite stamps for hot glass: stock geometric patterns (reticello, honeycomb, nautilus, flower of life) and custom logo stamps from 12 mm to about 3 in. They also make custom graphite and blow molds to order.
