Fantasilaboratoriet is a wooden mold and block shop in Sweden's Kingdom of Crystal, founded in 1998 by Gunnar Englund after he worked as a wooden mold maker at Orrefors from 1990 to 1997. It combines traditional mold turning with CAD/CAM, 3D scanning, and CNC milling, and also makes blocks, overlay and foot tools, and paddles.
