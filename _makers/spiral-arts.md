A Seattle shop that makes blowpipes, punties, and color tools, as well as furnaces, glory holes, and annealers for hot shops. It also repairs and re-heads pipes and punties, and publishes a [guide to steel vs. stainless pipes](https://spiralarts.com/pages/steel-vs-stainless).
