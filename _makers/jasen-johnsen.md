Jasen Johnsen is a glass sculptor who hand-makes carbon and stainless steel tools for glassblowing and hot sculpting: tweezers, tagli, crimps, and inside-sculpting tools, refined by using them in his own work. Every tool is one of a kind, and availability depends on his studio and teaching schedule. He also brings tools to the GAS Conference tech display.
