Putsch & Company makes glassblowing tools (shears, blowpipe heads, gathering balls, tongs, ladles, pucellas) mostly in the United States, with a few, like the Meniconi shears, from its subsidiary in Italy. It also customizes tools and repairs any brand. Glassblowing tools are a small part of a company that mostly builds sugar, panel-saw and filtration equipment.
