Jon Watanabe hand-forges jacks, shears, and tweezers to order at Soda Glass House, a working glass studio, and co-owner Alessia Arregui drafts and tests the designs. Many of the tools are modern takes on classic Italian, English, and German tools, such as Dino jacks and Putsch shears.
