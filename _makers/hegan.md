Scott Hegan's Hobbyglass began as a glassblowing studio in a two-car garage in 2003 and made its first optic mold in 2008. It now makes investment-cast silicon bronze optic molds and other tools in its Ukiah workshop. Everything is also sold through Olympic Color Rods.
