Shin Nagai's studio in a village near Mt. Fuji, making jacks, tweezers, pacioffi, and taglios in high-carbon steel. Maruko started showing abroad at the 2006 GAS conference in the US and now sells worldwide. Its jacks come with standard or oval blades in several blade lengths, strap widths, and handle lengths.
