Charlie Correll started blowing glass in 1971 and now blows glass and builds hot-shop equipment in Conway, Massachusetts. Best known for recuperative furnaces and burner systems, Correll also makes hot-shop tools: cork paddles, ladles, garage forks, and pastorales.
