Jakobssons Formverkstad, a mold shop in Sweden's glass kingdom founded by Thomas Jakobsson, who trained in the Orrefors mold shop. It's now run with his son Karl. It makes CNC-milled alder blocks and wooden molds, and in its own metal shop, blowpipes and punties. Nearly everything is made to order.
