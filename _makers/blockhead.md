Makes CNC-carved wooden blocks and molds from reclaimed Pacific Northwest bigleaf maple, with rot-resistant purpleheart handles and stainless hardware. It doesn't sell direct: the whole line is sold through Olympic Color Rods (Glass Color), and custom and left-handed blocks are made to order.
