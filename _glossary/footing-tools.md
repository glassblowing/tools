## What it is

Foot boards are pairs of wooden boards hinged together. Glass Toolbox's are planed and sanded cherry, hinged with two leather straps, and sized by the width of the foot they make, from 40 to 100 mm.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Fantlab makes a foot-shaping set of two profile blocks and a counter-block, mounted on stainless jack handles.{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

They shape the foot of a piece. Fantlab's set is for when "design and accurate repeatability is vital".{% include cite.html n=2 %} Like other wooden tools, foot boards are soaked before use and stored wet.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
