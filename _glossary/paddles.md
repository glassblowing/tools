## What it is

Paddles are flat pieces of wood or graphite.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Blockhead's wooden paddles are kiln-dried cherry, used wet or dry.{% include cite.html n=2 %} Metal versions exist too, like Hegan's 48"-long metal paddles.{% include cite.html n=3 %}

## How it's used

They're for making flat surfaces, such as the bottom of a piece.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Paddles also double as heat shields for the gaffer,{% include cite.html n="2,3" %} and long metal ones can serve as loading tools.{% include cite.html n=3 %}

Wooden paddles, like blocks, are often dipped in water while working; Blockhead says theirs can be soaked or used dry.{% include cite.html n=2 %}
