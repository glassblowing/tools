## What it is

Furnace glasses are safety glasses with lenses made for looking into hot glass. Phillips' light-green polycarbonate lens, for example, is made for furnace work, with UV and IR protection.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

Phillips recommends its light-green glasses for anyone working with glassblowing or other hot glass, with good color recognition for furnace work and good clarity.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Prescription versions are available by special order.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
