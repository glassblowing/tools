## What it is

Crow Print Tools' brushes are copper-bound fibre brushes on a sealed hemp handle, made round or flattened.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

They're for dusting and cleaning off color bar, garage components, and cane roll-ups, for furnace and borosilicate work alike.{% include cite.html n=1 %} They burn down slowly; as they do, you snip back the binding.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
