## What it is

Jim Moore's fin molds are stainless steel and come in six profiles (bowl, sherry, wine, pilsner, tumbler, and small tumbler), with 6 or 8 facets.{% include cite.html n=1 %} He also makes detachable fin molds with 18 ribs that can be arranged as needed.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

They're for faceted goblets and similar forms: "Once you learn to master it, your patience will be rewarded with crisp facets."{% include cite.html n=1 %}
