## What it is

A benchtop machine that holds and turns the pipe for threading. Spiral Arts' adjusts and locks into position, with a gauge for setting the angle;{% include cite.html n=1 %} Steinert's multi-positional threading wheel has replaceable bearings and bushings.{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

It's for spiral wraps of thread: the point, in Spiral Arts' words, is getting "that spiral wrap the same every time".{% include cite.html n=1 %}
