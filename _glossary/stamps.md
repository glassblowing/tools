## What it is

A stamp is a small patterned or figured die on a handle. Carlo Donà's Murano catalog includes stamp molds of faces, shells, skulls, lions, strawberries, cherubs and tigers, in sizes around 15–43 mm.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Jim Moore's texture stamps have a 1" head on a 5" handle in patterns like bullseye, honeycomb and fan.{% include cite.html n=2 %} Leviathan makes custom "3D brass stamp[s] or brand[s] for glass" up to 4" across.{% include cite.html n=3 %}

## How it's used

Pressed into a hot bit or prunt, a stamp leaves its figure or texture; Olympic Color Rods describes Jim Moore's as a way to "add some texture to your glass".{% include cite.html n=2 %} Custom stamps and brands put a maker's mark or logo on the work.{% include cite.html n=3 %}
