## What it is

Choppers are levered cutters. Spiral Arts' color bar chopper is about 18" × 13";{% include cite.html n=1 %} Hegan's murrini chopper cuts four 1/2" canes at a time, adjusts in height to chop color bar too, and has replaceable blades with four cutting edges each.{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

They break expensive color bar into usable chunks without wasting it{% include cite.html n=1 %} and cut cane into murrini. Hegan notes that how cleanly the ends cut depends on how well the cane or bar is annealed and how well its colors fit.{% include cite.html n=2 %}
