## What it is

The traditional shaping pad is a folded pad of water-soaked newspaper, roughly 15 cm (6") square and 1.3–2.5 cm (½–1") thick, held in the bare hand.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Makers now sell covers and alternatives: Standard Glass's Newspaper Koozie is a woven graphite-fiber cover for your own newspaper pad,{% include cite.html n=2 %} and Leviathan's Carbon Weave pads are dual-sided pads with a cotton insert.{% include cite.html n=3 %}

## How it's used

Wet newspaper shapes the piece much as a block does.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Covers like the Koozie are meant to extend the life of the newspaper and, in Hot Glass Color's words, prevent "smoke and ash from getting all up in your grill".{% include cite.html n=2 %}
