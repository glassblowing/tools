## What it is

These are steel rods ending in a ball or other shape. Jasen Johnsen's inside sculpting tools are 5/16" stainless rods with solid steel balls,{% include cite.html n=1 %} and Carlo Donà makes sculpting tools with balls from 5 mm to 50 mm.{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

Inside sculpting tools are for "sculpting from the inside of a bubble and pushing material out".{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## History

Carlo Donà's family foundry opened in Murano in 1923 making tools for Venetian glassworkers, and the craft has passed through four generations of blacksmiths.{% include cite.html n=2 %}
