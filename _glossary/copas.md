## What it is

Jim Moore makes two: the traditional copa and the triangular copa, both 12".{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

The traditional copa "will make an ordinary wrap into a perfect half-round", with the size depending on the wrap and the user; the triangular copa makes crisp triangular wraps.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Jim Moore notes that Augustin Fresnel found triangular wraps (prisms) have great optical properties.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
