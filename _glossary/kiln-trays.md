## What it is

Bugeye's kiln tray is a compartmentalized ceramic tray with eight cells.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

It keeps glass colors organized and contained in the kiln: chunks can't fall over or roll off the shelf, stay in order, and are corralled for easy pick-up on the blowpipe.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
