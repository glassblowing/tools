## What it is

Steinert's bit irons are 54" stainless rods with a 1/2" shaft and a 2" head, fitted with a grip and stop collar;{% include cite.html n=2 %} its solid gathering rods have a solid ball, such as 1.5" across, on the end of a 1/2" solid shaft.{% include cite.html n=3 %}

## How it's used

They're for gathering glass that isn't blown: Steinert calls its bit irons "great for quick bit work".{% include cite.html n=2 %} Murano's glass museum also lists solid iron rods used by the glass master's assistant to take glass out of the furnace.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
