## What it is

A ring of a set diameter on a handle. Jim Moore makes them in nine sizes from 1-1/8" to 2-7/8";{% include cite.html n=1 %} Leviathan GlassWorks makes them with replaceable rings from 1.5" to 5".{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

To make a sphere, the glassblower runs the ring around the bit as it rolls back and forth on the bench; after a few passes, in Jim Moore's words, "you'll have a new perfect marble".{% include cite.html n=1 %}
