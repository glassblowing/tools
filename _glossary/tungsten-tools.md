A length of tungsten held in a handle: Jasen Johnsen turns an aluminum handle and holds the tungsten in it with a set screw, in three diameters.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Carlo Donà also makes tungsten needles and a tungsten needle pincer.{% include cite.html n=2 %}
