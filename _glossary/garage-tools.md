## What it is

A garage fork is a long metal rod with a forked end.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Correll makes curved, straight, and "Avolio" forks, plus garage paddles.{% include cite.html n=3 %}

## How it's used

On Murano the *forcella* is used by the *forcellante* to load glassware into the annealing furnace and to move or remove it.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Corning's study of a 1570 painting of the Medici glass workshop describes the same job: placing finished pieces in the annealing oven with a long, forklike tool, then managing their gradual cooling by pushing them, in small steps, from the hottest to the coolest part of the chamber.{% include cite.html n=2 %}
