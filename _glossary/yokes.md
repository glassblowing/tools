## What it is

A yoke is a cradle for the pipe on a stand. Steinert's has two 1" balls each resting on smaller balls in a contoured seat, which gives it its smooth motion, on a shaft meant to be welded to a floor stand.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Spiral Arts' rolling yoke has transfer roller bearings, adjustable height, and casters, with a post for adding weight for big work.{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

The pipe rests and turns in the yoke while the glass is reheated.{% include cite.html n=3 %}

## History

In a 1570 painting of the Medici glass workshop, hooks at each workstation serve as yokes for the blowpipes and punties during reheating. The author of Corning's study of Renaissance Venetian glassworking notes that the same two-hook system was still used at every glassworking furnace on Murano until about thirty years ago.{% include cite.html n=3 %}
