## What it is

Cutting Edge Products describes its parrot-bill shear as "a lip shear with a point": it has the same cut and action as the company's [duckbill shears](/glossary/duckbill-shears/), but with a short, pointed blade.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

The point is for "digging a stone or bubble out of hot glass", reaching a flaw that a round-nosed trim shear can't get at.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
