## What it is

Bugeye's Twisty Bit is a bit that snaps onto a drill or a pulling handle.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

Bugeye loads several bits with cookies and keeps them hot in a kiln or garage at about 1000 degrees, then snaps on to one, attaches the cane mass, and twists or pulls the cane; after the pull the bit is quenched and released, ready for the next. It removes the wait for a post.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
