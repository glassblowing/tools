## What it is

A soffietta, or puffer, is a cone on the end of a tube. Jim Moore's come straight or bent, with cones from 2" to 4.5" across on a 17-1/4" tube;{% include cite.html n=1 %} Carlo Donà makes bent and straight puffers with cones from 50 to 150 mm.{% include cite.html n=3 %}

## How it's used

It's used to inflate a piece further once it has been taken off the blowpipe.{% include cite.html n=4 %} Jim Moore credits the puffer's ability to "thin out bulging walls and shoulders" with saving many pieces from the scrap heap.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Steinert's two-part aluminum puffer is meant for working alone, and its head can be detached and used with an air hose.{% include cite.html n=2 %}
