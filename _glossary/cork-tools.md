## What it is

Thick blocks of dense cork, usually 2" deep, with a handle: Correll makes cork paddles in 9" × 6" and 12" × 9",{% include cite.html n=1 %} and Glass Toolbox makes 200 × 150 × 50 mm cork blocks by hand in Wiltshire.{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

Cork's selling point is that it shapes glass without marking it: Correll sells its paddles "for mark free shaping",{% include cite.html n=1 %} and Glass Toolbox says its blocks let you shape your glass without leaving a mark, with handles for a secure grip.{% include cite.html n=2 %}
