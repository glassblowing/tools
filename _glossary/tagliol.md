## What it is

Murano's glass museum describes the *tagiol* as a rectangular metal paddle with a wooden handle and sharp edges.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Carlo Donà makes them with wood or stainless steel handles, in round-ended ("Pino") and square-ended shapes, in large and small sizes.{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

The glass master uses it to make small indentations or rows on the surface of the parison.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Sculpting versions are built heavier: BC Metalworking's taglio, based on glass artist John "Sleepy" Moran's, has a hardened and tempered spring-steel blade and a counterweight welded to the end of the handle.{% include cite.html n=3 %}
