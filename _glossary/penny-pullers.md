## What it is

Penny pullers are big shears with thick blades that won't flex. Cutting Edge Products' version is slightly larger than its Big Combo diamond shear, and the pipe grabber (the notch most [diamond shears](/glossary/diamond-shears/) have at the tip) has been left off.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

They were first made for cane pulling.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## History

Cutting Edge says its penny pullers weren't a big seller at first, but requests returned "with the surge in popularity of borosilicate glass tube production and making Murrini", and the company brought them back.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
