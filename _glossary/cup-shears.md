## What it is

Cup shears are small shears with short, slim blades and a small capacity. Jim Moore's cup shear, for example, has 1-5/8" blades and is "intended for use on intricate work where chomping through heavy material is not an issue".{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

They're made for stemware and other fine cutting, where the work is small and precise rather than heavy.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Jim Moore also makes a D2-steel version with thinner blades, which he says is tougher and "will make short work of boro or cool glass".{% include cite.html n=1 %}
