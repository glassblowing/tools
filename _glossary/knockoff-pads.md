## What it is

A pad of heat-resistant material that a finished piece is knocked off onto. Leviathan's Gnarly Knockoff Pads are a vermiculite-impregnated material rated to 2000°F.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

Leviathan points out that glass that has stopped moving is still around 1500°F, which is why a rated pad beats "old burnt pieces of gloves".{% include cite.html n=1 %}
