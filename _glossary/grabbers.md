## What it is

Grabbers have tines arranged to close on each other from opposite sides. Jasen Johnsen's version uses three stainless steel tines, which "grasp firmly and give the user control".{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

They're for "picking up parts from garage or oven" and are often used while assembling sculpture.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
