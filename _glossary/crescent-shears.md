## What it is

Watanabe Glass Tools' crescent shears are inspired by an antique pair in the maker's own collection, most likely from an early 20th-century American glassblowing factory.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## How it's used

They cut very cleanly and leave a different-shaped finish from traditional diamond shears; Watanabe uses them for cutting color bar, bits, and handles.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## History

When the maker came upon the antique pair, there was no modern version available, so he began making them.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
