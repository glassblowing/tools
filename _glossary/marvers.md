## What it is

A marver is a large flat table that the glass is rolled across.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Modern marvers are steel, typically stainless.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Smaller specialty plates exist too: Steinert's cane marver is an aluminum plate with a grooved surface on each side,{% include cite.html n=3 %} and its diamond marver is cast aluminum with a diamond pattern on one side.{% include cite.html n=4 %}

## How it's used

Rolling the glass on the marver shapes it and removes heat: the marver absorbs heat quickly and forms a stronger skin on the glass than a wooden tool does.{% include cite.html n=1 %}

## History

The name comes from marble, which marvers were originally made of.{% include cite.html n=1 %} On Murano the marver is the *bronzin*, from *bronzo*, bronze; the Conciatore lexicon notes that it was originally stone or marble, which is where the verb "to marver" comes from.{% include cite.html n=2 %}
