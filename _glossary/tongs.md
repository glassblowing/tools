## What it is

Put-away and loading tongs are long tongs with protected tips. Steinert's light put-away tongs are 20" of 1/4" stainless steel with high-temperature sleeves wired onto the ends;{% include cite.html n=1 %} Spiral Arts' heavier loading tongs have a 16" reach and replaceable covers of thick woven high-temperature fiber.{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

They carry work that's off the pipe: Steinert says its light tongs are useful for ornaments and for placing small work in the annealer,{% include cite.html n=1 %} and Spiral Arts calls its loading tongs "a must have for ornament and pumpkin makers".{% include cite.html n=2 %}
