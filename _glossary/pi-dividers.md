## What it is

A pi divider is a caliper with straight arms at one end and curved arms at the other.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Cutting Edge's is cut by CNC water jet from aluminum,{% include cite.html n=2 %} and Carlo Donà makes them in sizes from 110 to 315 mm.{% include cite.html n=3 %}

## How it's used

For cane pick-ups and roll-ups, the canes laid out on the plate have to wrap exactly around a collar, which means pi × diameter = circumference.{% include cite.html n=2 %} You measure the row of cane with the straight arms, and the curved end gives the size of collar needed.{% include cite.html n=1 %}
