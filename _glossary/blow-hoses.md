## What it is

A blow hose is a length of tubing with a mouthpiece, joined to the blowpipe by a swivel. Steinert's hose extension slides over the pipe's mouthpiece and has a 90-degree swivel connector on a 6-foot latex hose;{% include cite.html n=1 %} Glass Toolbox's kit uses a 200 cm silicone hose with three adapters and spare mouthpieces.{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

A hose lets you work a piece at the bench or glory hole without bringing the pipe to your mouth.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Glass Toolbox recommends its kit for working alone, schools, and busy studios.{% include cite.html n=2 %} Related tools solve the same problem in other ways: Spiral Arts' pocket inflator lets an assistant provide air at the bench without touching the mouthpiece,{% include cite.html n=3 %} and a rotating blowpipe extender fits on the end of the pipe for comfort and to reduce the risk of chipped teeth.{% include cite.html n=4 %}
