## What it is

Hot-shop protective clothing covers the parts of the body nearest the heat. Woven Kevlar sleeves come in 14" or 18" lengths, with or without a thumbhole;{% include cite.html n=1 %} mitts and gloves are made of a wool-lined carbon fiber and Kevlar blend;{% include cite.html n=2 %} and aluminized hoods, such as Chicago Protective Apparel's, give complete head and shoulder coverage with a replaceable Lexan window.{% include cite.html n=3 %}

## How it's used

Chicago Protective's hood sits far enough from the face to wear safety glasses or a mask underneath, and can be fitted with an air supply.{% include cite.html n=3 %}
