## What it is

A metal bowl on a long handle. Spiral Arts' have 4", 6", or 8" bowls on a 1-1/8" pipe about 60" long, with a T-handle.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Masters of Glass sells ladle bowls of 180 and 220 mm to weld onto your own handle.{% include cite.html n=2 %}

## How it's used

Ladles move molten glass: Spiral Arts lists casting into sand molds, emptying a furnace, and glazing a new crucible.{% include cite.html n=1 %} Mobile Glassblowing Studios makes its 6" ladle for emptying its small furnaces at the end of a session.{% include cite.html n=3 %}
